# Discord support evidence — 14 November 2026

Original public support screenshots preserved for the linked GitHub reports, with source and reporter credit in `sources.json`. Images are unchanged; rights remain with their original authors. This evidence branch is not an application asset or release input.

The preserved images were visually reviewed for credentials, personal paths and private logs. Images exposing personal paths or additional personal details were omitted; their reports are summarized separately without copying those details. A screenshot establishes what the reporter saw, not the cause or resolution of a defect.
