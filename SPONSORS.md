## Why sponsor?

VoiceStudio is free, fully local and AGPL-3.0. It has no paid tier, accounts or cloud service, so it has no recurring revenue. Sponsorship funds maintainer time, development tools, release infrastructure, signing certificates and test hardware.

Sponsorship does not change the software or model licence terms, guarantee features, or buy commercial support or a commercial licence.

---

## Sponsorship tiers

GitHub Sponsors is the checkout; its tier names and prices follow this table. Tiers are **recognition and communication**, not features or priority. Higher tiers include everything below them.

| Tier | Monthly | What you get |
|------|--------:|--------------|
| **Supporter** | $5+ | Funds development. Optional name in the supporter list below. |
| **Community Sponsor** | $25 | Your name with a link, in the README and in this file. |
| **Development Sponsor** | $100 | A small logo in the README and in this file, and a written development update each quarter (a summary, not a delivery commitment). |
| **Company Sponsor** | $500 | A logo and link on the [project website](https://voicestudio.sh). |
| **Strategic Sponsor** | $1,000 | A 30-minute feedback call each quarter. Feedback is welcome; it does not set the roadmap. |

The same tiers are published machine-readably in [`funding.json`](funding.json) ([funding.json](https://fundingjson.org) format); keep the two in step.

Recognition appears only on surfaces that exist today: this file, the README and the website. New surfaces are added to existing sponsors when they ship.

### How recognition works

- **Activation:** within five business days after your payment and assets are verified.
- **Monthly sponsors:** recognition lasts through the period you have paid for.
- **Annual arrangements:** the start and end dates are agreed in writing.
- **One-time donations:** a thank-you, not a permanent placement.
- **Anonymous support** is always welcome; you are listed only if you ask.
- Payment is verified privately before anything is published.

### Not covered by sponsorship

These are separate arrangements with their own terms, agreed privately:

- **Commercial licensing / Pro** has its own terms and checkout. Sponsoring does not purchase a licence.
- **Talks, workshops and featured advertising** are scoped individually, including timing, travel and frequency.

Existing sponsors keep the benefits they originally purchased.

## How to become a sponsor

**1. Pay.** No form is needed to support VoiceStudio:

- **GitHub Sponsors (monthly or one-time, recommended):** [github.com/sponsors/debpalash](https://github.com/sponsors/debpalash)
- **Ko-fi (recurring or one-time):** [ko-fi.com/debpalash](https://ko-fi.com/debpalash)

**2. Ask for recognition (optional).** If you want a listing or logo, open the [sponsorship inquiry](https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio/issues/new?template=sponsor.yml) with the public name, link and logo you want shown. **3. Private matters.** Receipts, invoices, annual or custom arrangements, and anything you don't want public go to [partner@voicestudio.sh](mailto:partner@voicestudio.sh). You can also ask in `#dev` on [Discord](https://discord.gg/bzQavDfVV9).

An inquiry is not a confirmed sponsorship: a tier is confirmed once payment is verified.

## Logo & asset guidelines

To make your logo look sharp everywhere (README on GitHub and the website), please send:

- **Format:** **SVG preferred** (scales cleanly); otherwise **PNG at 2× resolution**.
- **Background:** **transparent** — no baked-in white/black box.
- **Contrast:** send a variant that stays legible on **both light and dark** backgrounds, or one light-mode and one dark-mode file (GitHub and the app both render in either theme).
- **Dimensions:** legible at **~40px tall**; keep the wordmark within roughly **480px wide**. Landscape/wordmark shapes work best in the README row.
- **File size:** keep SVGs under ~50 KB and PNGs under ~100 KB.
- **Link target:** the destination URL you want the logo to point to (usually your homepage).

**How your logo gets added:**

- **Easiest:** attach the asset and link in your [sponsorship inquiry](https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio/issues/new?template=sponsor.yml) — the maintainer places it.
- **Or open a PR:** add your asset under `docs/sponsors/` and an entry to the matching section of this file.

By sponsoring you confirm you have the right to use the submitted logo and grant VoiceStudio permission to display it in the contexts above. We won't alter your logo beyond scaling, and we'll remove it promptly on request.
